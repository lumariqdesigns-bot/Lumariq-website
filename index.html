01 — About

Design that speaks
to what you feel.

At Lumariq, we believe the spaces around you shape how you feel — and that feeling is worth designing for. We translate emotion into atmosphere, intention into form, and vision into spaces that truly resonate.

Our work begins with clarity. We take the time to understand not just what you want a space to look like, but how you want it to feel — then we design every element to honour that intention.

From concept to final visualization, every project is precise, considered, and entirely yours. We don't follow trends. We follow the people who will live and move within the space.

04 — How We Work

Our process

I

Step 01

Discover

We begin by listening. Understanding your brief, your lifestyle, and the emotional quality you want the space to carry.

II

Step 02

Define

We translate your vision into a clear design direction — mood boards, spatial strategy, material palette, and conceptual intent.

III

Step 03

Design

Layouts, elevations, and detailed spatial compositions are developed — every proportion, texture, and light source considered.

IV

Step 04

Visualize

The design is brought to life through photorealistic renders, animated walkthroughs, or immersive 360° VR — so you can feel it before it exists.

V

Step 05

Deliver

Final design assets, presentations, and visualization files are packaged and handed over — complete, refined, and ready for your next step.
